# Kitchen Island: plan

A new island where children cook healthy recipes by following a recipe card:
take the ingredients out of the fridge and cupboard, measure them
("2 cups of oats", "1 tablespoon of baking powder", "150 grams of strawberries"),
cook, do one clean-up job, then serve a nice-looking dish to a friend. The
maths is counting, measuring (cups, spoons, ml, grams on a scale), fractions,
sharing equally, scaling a recipe and reading a timer.

Status: **built: all 5 recipes play, at all 4 age levels, in English and Hebrew** (see section 7). Not done yet: checking the quantities against the sources, the printable cards, final art, sounds and voice recordings.

### Decisions so far
- **Metric.** Cups and spoons first, with ml and grams beside them. Israeli measures: **1 cup = 240 ml, 1 tablespoon = 15 ml, 1 teaspoon = 5 ml**. Anything a recipe gives in grams is weighed on a kitchen **scale** (a new tool, and good maths: reading a dial or display). Oven in °C.
- **Recipes from Israeli sites** (below).
- **The island is fully open:** every recipe is there for every child from the start, with no locks and no age band filter. The difficulty follows the child's age instead (section 3).
- **One clean-up job per round.**
- **Five recipes**, including a simple hot dog.
- **A real recipe card** for families, but only for a recipe whose quantities a person has checked against the source page (section 5).

### 🍔 Turkey Oat Burgers
Source: [Sweet Scent – קציצות הודו עם שיבולת שועל](https://sweetscent.co.il/turkey-meatballs-with-oats-recipe-2/). The source makes 20 meatballs, or 30 small ones; we shape the same mixture into burgers.

| Ingredient | Source amount | Kept in |
|---|---|---|
| Ground turkey breast | 500 g | fridge |
| Fine oats | 100 g | cupboard |
| Onion | 1 medium (120 g) | cupboard |
| Carrot | 1 small (80 g) | fridge |
| Garlic | 2 cloves | cupboard |
| Egg (L) | 1 | fridge |
| Olive oil | 25 ml | cupboard |
| Whole-wheat buns, lettuce, tomato | for serving (ours) | cupboard / fridge |

Also on the page (on the card, not in the game): 3 tbsp cold water as needed, 20 g chopped parsley, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp sweet paprika, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp baking soda (optional). Serves 6. The page fries the onion first in 1 tbsp of the oil, rests the mixture 25–30 minutes, then **bakes at 190 °C for 20–22 minutes** (turning after 12), or fries 3–4 minutes a side. It writes the oil as "25 ml (2 tablespoons)". Nice maths: **25 ml of oil = 5 teaspoons**, or 1 tablespoon + 2 teaspoons; 500 g shared into 5 burgers = 100 g each.
✅ Checked. Still open: burgers are bigger than the page's meatballs, so they may need longer in the oven than 20–22 minutes.

### 🌭 Homemade Chicken Hot Dogs (5 to 8): the simple one
Source: [וואלה אוכל – נקניקיות עוף פטנט ביתיות לילדים](https://food.walla.co.il/item/3111815) (Avi Biton's recipe). Lean chicken breast, with nothing processed.

| Ingredient | Source amount | Kept in |
|---|---|---|
| Chicken breast | 500 g | fridge |
| Small onion | 1 | cupboard |
| Garlic | 2 cloves | cupboard |
| Ketchup | 1 tablespoon | fridge |
| Eggs | 2 | fridge |
| Cornflour | 1½ teaspoons (the summary said 2 tablespoons) | cupboard |
| Soy sauce | 2 tablespoons (the summary said 1) | cupboard |
| Oil | 1 tablespoon (+ a little for searing) | cupboard |
| Rolls, mustard, ketchup | for serving | cupboard |

Method: blend everything smooth → roll into sausages in cling film → simmer about **15 minutes** on low heat → cool in ice water → sear in a pan → into rolls. Eggs: 2 medium or 1 large. The **game keeps it simple**: blend → roll 5 sausages → pot, then pan (grown-up helper) → each into a roll → toppings, with cucumber and tomato on the side. The real card will say what the safety note in the search results says: **for children under 5, cut hot dogs lengthwise into thin strips, not rings** (choking risk).
✅ Checked. The page says plain "rolls"; whole-wheat would be our suggestion on the card.

**"Healthy" without labelling food bad.** The game never calls a food bad or junk. The healthy part is in the recipes: fruit, vegetables, oats, lean meat, little or no added sugar. The extra items in the fridge and cupboard are other recipes' ingredients, not candy to resist.

**Safety habits built in:** every recipe starts with *wash your hands*. Hot and sharp steps (oven, stove, pot, knife) show a grown-up helper, a 🧑‍🍳 hand that does that part while the child taps, and the spoken line says "Ask a grown-up to help." Raw egg and raw meat → wash hands again before serving.

## 2. How a game goes: the stations

One scene, `KitchenScene` (`static plays = ['Kitchen']`). Each recipe is data that lists its steps, so the scene runs them in order. The camera pans along one long kitchen: **fridge and cupboard → counter → stove/oven → sink → table**.

| # | Station | What the child does | Maths | Reuses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Recipe card** | Sees the picture recipe (ingredient, amount, tool) and hears it read | reading amounts | Narrator, RulesCard |
| 1 | **Wash hands** | Scrubs under the tap until the bubbles fill up | — | coverage (`coverAt`/`coverageRatio`) |
| 2 | **Gather** | Opens the fridge and cupboard and drags what the card shows to the counter. Counted things must match (2 eggs). A wrong item gets a gentle "That's not in our recipe" and goes back | counting, matching | new `Pantry` (finite items, unlike the endless `DragTray`) |
| 3 | **Measure** | **Spoons and cups:** pick the tool and scoop; the bowl fills and a tally shows the scoops. **ml:** pour into a jug up to the line. **Grams:** put things on the scale until the needle reaches the number | units, fractions, reading a scale, repeated addition | new `MeasureStation`, `Scale` |
| 4 | **Prepare** | Mash, crack, stir/whisk (circle drag), cut (swipe, like the pizza cuts), shape into N equal burgers or sausages | halves, equal sharing | `isCut`, stir gesture |
| 5 | **Cook** | Oven, stove, pot or blender, with the grown-up helper. The child sets the temperature (180 °C) and timer (20 min) to the card's numbers | reading numbers on a dial | new `TimerDial` |
| 6 | **Clean-up job** (one, while it cooks) | One of: put everything away (**cold → fridge, dry → cupboard**), dishes into the sink, or wipe the counter until it shines. It changes from round to round. The timer ringing means the food is done | sorting, a countdown | `Pantry` (reversed), coverage |
| 7 | **Serve & decorate** | Plate it and decorate: blueberries on each muffin, a strawberry on each glass, building the burger, toppings on the hot dog. A friend (`CUSTOMERS`) eats it, and celebrate() | counting, order | DragTray, Juice |

**Points** (`finishRound`): gathered right first time, measured right first time, clean-up job done before the timer rang, served as the card asked. 4 points a recipe.
**Hints:** `IdleHint` points at the next ingredient, door or tool. After a second wrong scoop, the right tool glows. The timer only paces the clean-up; nobody loses to it.

## 3. One open island, difficulty by age

**Five skills, one per recipe**, all with `prereqs: []`. The island shows all of them to every child. Each round picks its difficulty from the **child's age band** at play time (`kitchenLevel(band)` in `logic/kitchen.js`), so a 4-year-old and a 9-year-old make the same muffins at different levels:

| Band | Measuring | Extra maths |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 3-4 | Counts only: whole things and number of scoops ("2 cups" = 2 scoops with the cup). The scale and jug show a big **target mark** to fill to | count to 10, one into each muffin cup |
| 🧭 5-6 | Cups and spoons by name (cup vs tablespoon vs teaspoon); ½ shown as a picture; scale to a round number (100 g, 200 g) | cut in halves, timer to 20 |
| 🧱 7-8 | ½ cup, tablespoon vs teaspoon; ml on the jug (250); any grams (25 g, 150 g) | 25 ml = 5 teaspoons; 500 g into 5 burgers |
| 🏆 9-10 | One-tool challenges (2 cups with only the ½ cup = 4 scoops); **double or halve** the recipe; grams ↔ cups for oats | scale for a party of 4 or 6 |

No age band filter for this island means one small rule change: `skillsFor` (or the island screen) skips the band filter for a subject marked `open: true` in `subjects.js`. The World Map and Parent Dashboard count its 5 skills for every band.

## 5. The real recipe card (for families)

- In the Parent Dashboard (behind the grown-up gate): **🍳 Recipe cards**. Each card shows the dish, the **source's exact quantities** (not the game's age-level versions), the method in short steps, the safety notes (oven, knife, hot dogs for under-5s) and the source link. It can be printed or saved with `services/exportFile.js`.
- **The verification gate:** a recipe file's `verified` is `{ date, by, checkedUrl }`. Only verified recipes show a card; the rest say "Card coming soon". `npm test` enforces it.
- **Verified:** every recipe was checked against its source page on 2026-11-16 (see section 1). A recipe whose source changes, or a new recipe, needs the same check before its card shows.
